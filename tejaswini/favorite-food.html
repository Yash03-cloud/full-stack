panipuri

Panipuri is a snack associated with the cuisines of the Indian subcontinent consisting of a deep-fried spherical puri shell, hollowed out for a filling and dipped in flavoured waters. Panipuri is primarily a street food and is part of the chaat category of light snacks.i

momos

Momos are a type of steamed, filled dumpling in Nepalese and Tibetan cuisine, also popular in neighbouring Bangladesh, Bhutan, and India. They are usually served with a dipping sauce known as achar or chutney. They can also be served in soup, such as jhol momo or mokthuk.
