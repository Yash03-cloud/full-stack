My Favourite Food

shahi paneer

shahi Paneer (pronounced puh-neer) is a fresh, unaged, non-melting soft cheese deeply rooted in South Asian cuisine. Often referred to as Indian cottage cheese, it is completely vegetarian and famous for its mild flavor and texture that holds its shape even when cooked, grilled, or fried

misal pav

Misal Pav is an iconic, fiery street food and breakfast dish originating from the Indian state of Maharashtra. The word "Misal" translates to "mixture" in Marathi, which perfectly describes the dish: a complex combination of textures, spice levels, and ingredients layered together. It is traditionally eaten with Pav, which are soft, pillowy Indian bread rolls.

fried rice

Fried rice is a globally beloved, ultra-versatile dish made from stir-frying cooked rice in a wok or frying pan, typically tossed with eggs, vegetables, seafood, or meat. Emerging from Ancient China as a clever, delicious way to prevent food waste by reviving leftover rice, it has evolved into a staple comfort food across countless global cultures.
